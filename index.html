Do This Today (First 24–48 Hours)

Isolate affected field patches immediately. Spray recommended Propiconazole 25% EC or fermented sour buttermilk early in the morning before dew condensation to curb airborne fungal spore dispersal.

Organic & Biological Management

Eco-friendly & residue-free treatments

Spray 5% Cow urine solution or Sour buttermilk (Chhachh) mixed with copper vessel extract (50ml/L); apply Trichoderma harzianum (10g/L).

Chemical Control Formulation

Targeted fungicide / pesticide prescription

Spray Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1 ml/L or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1 ml/L upon first detection.

Pesticide & Spray Application Safety Guidelines

🛡️ Wear personal protective gear: rubber gloves, safety face mask, and full-sleeve protective clothing.
🌤️ Avoid spraying during strong winds, rainy forecasts, or midday heat (spray early morning 6–9 AM or late afternoon).
⏳ Strictly adhere to the Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) stated on the product label before harvesting produce.
♻️ Triple-rinse, puncture and safely dispose empty containers; never reuse for household or animal feed purposes.

Long-Term Cultural & Preventive Practices

Sow rust-resistant varieties like HD-2967, PBW-550, or DBW-187; avoid late sowing; avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer.

How KrishiMitra Works: Expert System Architecture

1. Dual Inference Engines

Forward Chaining (Data-Driven): Evaluates observed plant symptoms step-by-step from working memory to match condition premises and infer the most probable disease candidates.
Backward Chaining (Goal-Driven): Hypothesizes high-priority diseases first based on the chosen crop, then queries targeted premise symptoms to systematically confirm or refute each hypothesis.

2. MYCIN Certainty Factor Algebra

When multiple diagnostic rules fire confirming the same disease, KrishiMitra combines evidence using the classic MYCIN certainty combination formula:

CF_combined = CF_current + CF_new × (1 - CF_current)

For example, if Rule 1 provides CF = 0.88 and Rule 2 provides CF = 0.82, the combined certainty becomes:
0.88 + 0.82 × (1 - 0.88) = 0.88 + 0.0984 = 0.9784 (98% Certainty).
